Meeting Transcriptor
====================

Records your meetings and writes down what is said, on your own computer.
No account, no subscription, nothing is uploaded.

HOW TO START
1. Unzip the whole folder somewhere (for example Documents). Keep all the files together.
2. Double-click MeetingTranscriptor.exe.
3. If Windows shows "Windows protected your PC", click "More info" and then "Run anyway".
4. The first time, choose where your meetings should be saved.
5. The first start needs internet for a few minutes to download the speech model.
   After that the app works offline.

HOW TO USE
- Press "Start recording" before or during a call, or press Ctrl+Alt+R,
  or say "start recording". Say "stop recording" to finish.
- It hears your microphone (You) and whatever plays through your speakers (Others).
  Nothing joins the meeting.
- Headphones give the cleanest result.
- Settings: choose your microphone, language and save folder.

NEEDS
- Windows 10 or 11 (64-bit).
- Tell people when you record them. In many places that is required by law.
